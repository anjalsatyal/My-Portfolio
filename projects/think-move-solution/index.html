Project overview

This was the first video for Think Move Solution (TMS). Its purpose was to introduce the agency, strengthen brand awareness and attract potential clients. These were the project’s intentions; no measured campaign results are claimed here.

In practice

Creative approach & execution.

The concept and script centered on introducing TMS. This gave the piece a specific task: establish an agency’s presence in a fast-moving social feed.

02

My role included ideation, scripting and shooting, followed by editing. Production and post shaped a visual language designed for social platforms.

The available final video is a 13-second landscape edit. It is presented here in its original format, as the concrete output of the project.
